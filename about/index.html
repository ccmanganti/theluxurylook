The Luxury Look is an interior design and home staging firm with a focus on spaces that feel personal, beautiful and intentional.

Founder and CEO Kristine Vowles established the firm in 2013, building on extensive experience in real estate and design. The company works across residential interiors, staging, Airbnb spaces and corporate environments.

Our approach pairs attentive collaboration with the details that make a room unforgettable.
