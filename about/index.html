01 / The philosophy

We don't begin with a trend.
We begin with you.

The Luxury Look draws on a rare combination of interior design and luxury real estate experience. Our approach brings visual clarity to the practical realities of a space, whether it's the home you live in or the property you're preparing for its next chapter.

Good design should create a feeling - and make sense for the people who use it.

02 / Meet the founder

A designer's eye.
A real estate perspective.

Kristine Vowles / Founder & CEO

Kristine established The Luxury Look in 2013, following an extensive career in real estate. Her understanding of property, presentation and the people behind each decision informs the way she approaches design.

She listens for what matters most to clients, bringing that vision into focus through a balance of function, beauty and individual character.
