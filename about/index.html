01 / THE STORY

Built on experience.
Driven by imagination.

Kristine founded The Luxury Look in 2013 after a long career in luxury real estate, brokerage leadership and property development. Her experience has shaped an approach that blends considered design with a practical understanding of how spaces are lived in, experienced and presented.

From real estate
to real spaces.

Before founding The Luxury Look in 2013, Kristine held real-estate and business leadership roles with organizations including Prudential Real Estate, Westar Development, Coldwell Banker and Keller Williams. Working with agents, homeowners, buyers and development teams gave her a deep understanding of the people and priorities behind each property. That background still informs her decisions, whether the brief is a personal residence, a major renovation or a home preparing for market.

The philosophy behind the work.

Kristine believes successful interiors start with listening. She considers how a room needs to function, how people should feel when they enter it, and what will make the space distinctly their own. Her work brings together architecture, layout, furnishings, texture, art and finishing details with an eye for both beauty and practicality. She welcomes collaboration with homeowners, architects, builders and fellow design professionals, turning a client’s ideas into a deliberate, cohesive direction.
